MIT undergrad programs: what's on OpenCourseWare

All 55 MIT undergraduate programs plus the core requirements, with every course listed on each program's official degree chart, checked against MIT OpenCourseWare's full course list. Data pulled directly from catalog.mit.edu and ocw.mit.edu. Last updated: …. Course lists include elective options, so programs with long elective lists show many "not on OCW" courses even when the required core is covered.

"Other number" means OCW has the class under its number before MIT's 2022 renumbering (e.g. 6.006 for 6.1210), or a class with the same title under a different number. "Not on OCW" means no matching course exists in OCW's full course list. "More free materials" lists other free sources we verified (MIT course websites, MITx courses on edX that are free to audit), plus quick searches of MIT Learn (which covers OCW, MITx and MIT Open Learning Library) and YouTube. OCW materials are free under Creative Commons BY-NC-SA. Full requirements: catalog.mit.edu/degree-charts.
